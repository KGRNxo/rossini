# Rossini OS — Product

Assumptions below were inferred from the written project brief (no interview possible in this session).

- **Product**: operating system for e-ink readers; the Shell is the first thing seen after boot.
- **Mode**: Operate. The reader wants to resume reading within one tap and manage a library without friction.
- **Use scene**: handheld, one hand, often lying down or travelling; ambient light varies from dark rooms to sun. E-ink: no animation, slow redraw, 16 grays at best.
- **Tone**: calm, quiet, German first (English available). Controls name their action. No marketing voice.
- **Anti-references**: Kindle's storefront-first home; desktop metaphors; GPU glass effects; ornamental icons.
- **Principles**: black on white with gray steps; hierarchy through size and weight, not boxes; one primary action per screen;
  touch targets >= 9 mm (about 105 px at 300 ppi); never redraw what did not change.
